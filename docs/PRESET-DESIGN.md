# Factory preset design

The factory bank is intentionally guitar-first. There are 108 curated presets and only six dedicated synth presets:

- Neon Blade
- FM Machine
- Glass Circuit
- Pulse Forge
- Sub Horizon
- Pluck Reactor

The guitar bank is divided across clean, crunch/rock, modern metal, djent/rhythm, death/extreme, progressive, lead, ambient, sitar/world and special categories.

Presets are shaped around gain staging, cabinet/mic choices, low/high-pass filtering, drive/boost combinations and musical playing use rather than random parameter permutations.
